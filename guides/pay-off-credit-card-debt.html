How to Pay Off Credit Card Debt Step by Step

Easy Budget Tracker guides · Updated October 22, 2026

Credit card interest can make balances grow quickly, so paying only the minimum keeps you in debt for a long time. A clear plan helps. These steps work for most people.

1. List every balance

For each card, write the current balance, the minimum payment, the due date and the interest rate shown on your statement. Include installment plans and other loans if you want the full picture.

2. Stop adding new charges

It is hard to climb out while still digging. Use cash or debit for everyday spending while you pay down the balance, or limit the card to a small planned amount you pay in full.

3. Always pay at least the minimum, on time

Late payments can add fees and may affect your credit record. Set a reminder a few days before each due date.

4. Choose a payoff method

MethodHow it worksBest for
AvalanchePut extra money on the card with the highest interest rate firstPaying the least interest overall
SnowballPut extra money on the smallest balance firstQuick wins that keep you motivated

Either method works. The best one is the one you will stick with. Pay the minimum on all other cards while you focus on one.

5. Find the extra money

Look at your flexible categories for a few weeks and trim what you can. In Easy Budget Tracker, you can list each card as its own line, set a monthly payment, and see how much is left in the month.

6. Check before converting or transferring

Balance transfers and installment conversions can lower your payment, but they may add fees or extend the time you pay. Compare the total cost, not just the monthly amount.

7. Keep a small buffer

A small emergency fund stops the next surprise from landing on the card again.

General information, not financial advice. Check your own card terms for interest and fees.
