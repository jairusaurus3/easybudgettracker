Easy Budget Tracker

What you can do with Easy Budget Tracker

  • Plan each month: add income, bills, spending, savings, credit cards and loans, and see your remaining balance at a glance.
  • Repeating bills: enter a bill once and change only the months that differ.
  • Credit card bill reminders: set a due day, see a banner before it is due, and add the due dates to your phone calendar.
  • Spending limits: set an overall limit and limits for each spending category, with a warning as you near them.
  • Savings goals and a what-if savings calculator: see what saving a little extra each month adds up to, and how long a savings target will take.
  • Track money owed to you and plan shopping with a grocery list that has its own spending limit.
  • Private backup: your budget stays in your browser. Export to Excel or CSV, or sign in with Google to keep a copy in your own Drive.

A simple monthly budget planner

Easy Budget Tracker helps you see where your money goes each month. Add your income, bills, spending, savings, credit cards and loans, set limits, and check what is left. Bills can repeat every month, and you only change the months that differ. Your budget is saved in your own browser, and you can back it up or sign in with Google to keep a copy in your own Drive.
