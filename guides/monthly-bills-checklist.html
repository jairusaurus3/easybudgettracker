Monthly Bills Checklist: Never Miss a Payment

Easy Budget Tracker guides · Updated October 22, 2026

Late fees and service interruptions are expensive and usually avoidable. A written list of every recurring bill, with its due date, is the easiest fix. Use this checklist to make sure nothing is missing.

Housing

  • Rent, boarding fee or mortgage amortization
  • Association dues or building fees
  • Property tax or home insurance, if you pay them yourself

Utilities and connectivity

  • Electricity
  • Water
  • Internet
  • Mobile plan or prepaid load
  • Cooking gas

Transport

  • Commute costs, fuel or parking
  • Vehicle loan, registration and insurance

Debt and credit

  • Credit card payments
  • Personal, car, home or phone installment loans
  • Buy-now-pay-later plans

Insurance, school and family

  • Health, life or other insurance premiums
  • Tuition and school fees
  • Allowance or support you send to family

Subscriptions

  • Streaming, music, cloud storage, apps, memberships

Contributions and taxes you pay yourself

  • Any government contributions or taxes that are not deducted from your pay, for example if you are self-employed

Habits that prevent late payments

  1. Write the due date next to every bill. Due dates are often different from the date you get paid.
  2. Set a reminder a few days early. Easy Budget Tracker can remind you of upcoming card due dates and export them to your calendar.
  3. Pay bills right after payday when you can, so the money is not spent elsewhere first.
  4. Review subscriptions every quarter. Cancel the ones you no longer use.

Once your bills are listed, set monthly limits for flexible spending. See how to track expenses as a beginner.
