How to Track Your Expenses: A Beginner Guide

Easy Budget Tracker guides · Updated October 22, 2026

Tracking expenses sounds like a chore, but the idea is simple: know where your money goes so you can decide where it should go. You do not need to record every coffee to get value from it.

Step 1: Pick a few categories

Keep it short. Groceries, transport, eating out, utilities, rent, health, fun and savings cover most of life. Too many categories make tracking tiring and you will quit.

Step 2: Decide how detailed to be

You can log each purchase, or you can enter one total per category at the end of the week or month. Monthly totals are enough to spot trends, and they take minutes. Easy Budget Tracker uses monthly totals per category for this reason.

Step 3: Set a limit for each flexible category

A limit turns a number into a decision. If groceries are ₱6,000 and you are at ₱5,000 with ten days left, you can adjust early.

Step 4: Do a weekly check-in

Pick one day and spend five minutes comparing what you spent with your limits. Small corrections each week are easier than a big correction at the end of the month.

Step 5: Review each month

Compare this month with previous months. Which category grew? Which one shrank? Ask whether the change was a one-off or a new habit.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting cash and small purchases
  • Setting limits that are too tight to follow
  • Never reviewing the numbers
  • Leaving out irregular costs such as annual fees, gifts and repairs

Once you can see your spending, the next step is to build a plan. Try the 50/30/20 rule as a starting framework.

General information, not financial advice.
