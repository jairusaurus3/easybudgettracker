Privacy Policy

Easy Budget Tracker · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Easy Budget Tracker is a personal budgeting web app. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

What the app stores

The budget you enter (income, bills, savings, loans, monthly limits, and settings) is saved in your own web browser on your device. If you choose Google sign-in, a backup is saved to your own Google Drive app-data folder. The app does not send budget amounts or category names to the developer.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

If you choose Sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to use the Google Drive app data scope (drive.appdata). This lets the app save and load a backup copy of your budget in a hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive. The app cannot see or access your other Drive files.

The app does not read your name, email address, contacts, or any other Google account information. Sign-in is optional; the app works without it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or share your personal or financial data.
  • This version does not display ads. If advertising is introduced later, this policy must be updated before it is enabled.
  • We do not have access to your budget or your Google Drive.

Anonymous usage counts

To understand general usage, the app uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counting service that does not use cookies. It counts anonymous page views of the app and the guide pages, and a small set of fixed event labels, for example: first visit on a device, returning after 7 or 30 days (a simple flag stored on your device), signing in with Google, entering a first budget amount, setting a budget limit, using the savings or goal calculators, exporting a backup, calendar, Excel or CSV file, finishing a file import, opening the Transactions, Reports, Collectibles, Grocery list, Insights or How to use pages, adding or ticking a first entry on them, and setting a shopping limit. Files you import are read on your device and are never uploaded. Your collectibles and grocery lists are saved together with your budget on your device, and in your own Google Drive backup if you sign in. They are not shared with anyone. These events contain only the fixed label. They never include your name, email address, Google account information, budget amounts, category names, or other financial records. GoatCounter may process technical details such as the page address, referrer, browser and country in aggregate form.

Your control

You can sign out of Google inside the app at any time. You can delete your data by using Erase and start blank in the app's Settings, by clearing your browser's site data, and by revoking the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access also lets you remove the app's saved data from your Drive.

Fonts

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads. No budget data is sent.

Hosting

The app is hosted on Netlify, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and page requests) when you load the page. That is separate from your budget data.
