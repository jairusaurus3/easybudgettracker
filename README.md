Easy Budget Tracker

A simple month-by-month budgeting web app with dedicated groceries and collectibles tracking. Installable on your phone, with credit card due-date reminders and backup to your own Google Drive.

🌐 Live site: https://ezbudgettracker.app

Features
Monthly budgeting: track income and expenses month by month
Groceries: keep tabs on your grocery spending
Collectibles: track spending on your collectibles
Credit card due-date banner: see upcoming due dates at a glance
Calendar export: add due dates to your calendar
Installable app (PWA): add it to your phone's home screen
Google Drive backup: sign in with Google and your budget is saved in your own Drive
Privacy

Your budget data is saved in your own Google Drive using the app data folder only. The app cannot see your other Drive files.
